# Monash: 0047 vs 4585 vs M6046 — what they actually are and how to choose

Companion to `monash-phd-pathway.md`. Written 25 August 2026.

## First, the thing that makes this confusing

These are not three versions of the same degree. They are three different *species* of
qualification, and they answer different questions:

- **0047 — Doctor of Philosophy (MNHS).** A pure research degree. Makes you a *researcher*.
- **4585 — Doctor of Philosophy (Clinical Psychology).** A research degree with an accredited
  professional program built into it. Makes you a *researcher and a clinical psychologist*.
- **M6046 — Master of Clinical Psychology.** A coursework professional degree. Makes you a *clinical
  psychologist*.

Once you see it that way, the intake difference stops being a quirk and starts being the clue. 0047
takes students all year round because a research degree is just you, a supervisor and a project —
there is no cohort to assemble and no teaching calendar to fit. 4585 and M6046 are February-only
because they run fixed coursework sequences and sequenced clinical placements in partner health
services. You cannot join a placement pipeline in the middle. Monash confirms this directly for the
clinical doctorates: "Yes, there is only one intake for the Clinical PhD programs, with commencement
in February each year."

So the intake pattern is telling you which ones are professional training programs and which one is
research.

## Entry requirements: this is the decisive part

Something counterintuitive falls out of the numbers. **The master's has the highest academic bar of
the three, and it is the one you do not currently meet.**

- **M6046** requires an APAC-accredited four-year sequence "with an overall average of 80%", broken
  down as a three-year undergraduate major at 80% *and* a fourth-year specialisation at 80%. At a
  WAM of 70–75 you are below the published threshold, and this is a competitive program where
  meeting the minimum is not sufficient anyway. Realistically this door is closed at Monash right
  now.
- **4585** requires a three-year APAC-accredited sequence plus an APAC-accredited fourth year at
  "Honours 1 or 2A". H2A is 70–79, so you are inside the requirement. But this is the most
  competitive program in the School, with a small cohort, a video statement, three referee reports
  and an interview. Eligible is not the same as likely.
- **0047** requires H2A, which you meet, and admission is driven by whether a supervisor wants to
  take you on. It is by far the most accessible of the three.

So the ranking by how realistic each is for you, today, is the reverse of what you might expect:
0047, then 4585, then M6046.

**Verify:** that Monash accepts its own GDPA as satisfying the "fourth year" requirement for each
course. It should — the GDPA is APAC-accredited, includes a supervised research project, and Monash
Online markets it as the pathway into exactly these programs, and other providers explicitly name
"Honours-equivalent Graduate/Postgraduate Diploma in Psychology" in the same requirement. But the
wording on the Monash pages says "Honours degree (or equivalent)", and you want that "or equivalent"
confirmed for your specific case in writing rather than assumed.

## Money: a much bigger gap than it looks

This is where the choice has real financial consequences, and it runs opposite to intuition.

**0047 and 4585 are graduate research courses.** The 4585 course page states it plainly: "This course
is supported by the Australian government's Research Training Program (RTP). Under the RTP, domestic
students are awarded an RTP Fees Offset which covers the cost of course tuition fees." Combined with
RTP Scholarship Procedure 2.12 — "all domestic students at the University will receive the RTP Fees
Offset" — that means **tuition is $0** for you as a domestic student, automatically, for both
doctorates. On top of that, both make you eligible to be considered for a stipend of $37,145/yr
(2026 rate), tax-free for full-time candidates.

**M6046 is a coursework master's.** It is not RTP-supported. A Commonwealth Supported Place means the
government subsidises part of the cost and you pay a student contribution, deferrable through
HECS-HELP — far better than full fee, but it is still a debt rather than zero, and there is no
stipend. Placements are unpaid.

So the comparison for a domestic student is roughly:

- 4585: tuition $0, possibly paid $37k/yr for 3.5 years
- 0047: tuition $0, possibly paid $37k/yr for 3–4 years
- M6046: you pay a student contribution for 2 years, earn nothing, and do unpaid placements

If you are choosing between 4585 and M6046 purely on money, 4585 wins by a very wide margin. That is
unusual and worth sitting with: the longer, more prestigious qualification is also the cheaper one.

**Verify:** the exact student contribution band and amount for M6046, and whether all offers are CSP
or whether some are full-fee. Ask directly how CSP places are allocated — do not assume an offer is
Commonwealth supported.

## What each one actually qualifies you to do

This is the part that matters most for your career and it is easy to get wrong.

**0047 does not make you a psychologist.** A research PhD is not an APAC-accredited program of study.
Your GDPA gives you eligibility for *provisional* registration, and a research PhD does not advance
that. If you complete GDPA + 0047 you will hold a doctorate and be a credible researcher, but you
will not be a registered psychologist and will not be able to practise. To register later you would
still need an accredited fifth-and-sixth-year program, or the 5+1 pathway. This is the single most
important thing to be clear about before choosing 0047.

**4585 and M6046 both lead to general registration**, and then to endorsement as a clinical
psychologist via a registrar program. But the registrar requirements differ by qualification, and
this is set by the Psychology Board of Australia, not by Monash:

| Qualification | Minimum practice duration | Registrar hours | Supervision | CPD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Master's approved to sixth year (M6046) | 88 weeks | 3,000 | 80 hrs | 80 hrs |
| Approved to sixth year with a doctoral thesis (4585) | 66 weeks | 2,250 | 60 hrs | 60 hrs |
| Approved to seventh year or above (DPsych/PsyD) | 44 weeks | 1,500 | 40 hrs | 40 hrs |

4585 sits in the middle row, which is why the Monash page quotes 2,250 hours. So the doctorate saves
you 750 registrar hours and 22 weeks compared with the master's. Note that the registrar program is
*paid employment* as a psychologist, not more study — so this is a difference in how long you spend
on a lower supervised salary, not in how long you spend paying fees.

## Time to being qualified

Rough full-time minimums, from starting to being an endorsed clinical psychologist:

- **M6046:** 2 years study + 88 weeks registrar ≈ **3.7 years**. No PhD.
- **4585:** 3.5 years study + 66 weeks registrar ≈ **4.8 years**. Plus a PhD.
- **0047:** 3.5 years ≈ PhD only. Not a psychologist, and adding registration afterwards would mean
  a further accredited program.

The master's is the fastest route to practising. The clinical PhD costs roughly one extra year and
gives you a doctorate and a research training record for it. The research PhD is the fastest route
to a research career and a dead end for clinical practice unless you add another degree later.

## Career pathways

**After 0047.** Postdoctoral researcher, then fellowship, then group leader — the academic track.
Also research fellow roles in institutes, clinical trial coordination and management, biostatistics
and data-heavy health roles, government and policy analysis, and industry research in digital health
and medical devices. Given your interest in app-based cognitive assessment, industry is a real
option: companies like Cogstate hire PhD-level scientists. What you will not have is any ability to
see clients. Your income early on is postdoc salary, and academic careers are competitive and
grant-dependent.

**After 4585.** The clinician-scientist track, and the broadest set of options of the three. You can
practise clinically, including private practice at clinical-psychologist Medicare rebate rates; you
can run a research program; you can work in clinical trials where a registered clinician who
understands methodology is unusually valuable; and you can move into digital therapeutics and
health-tech, which is precisely where "app-based technologies to personalise treatments" lives
commercially. It also hedges: if academia does not work out, you have a licensed profession to fall
back on, and clinical work is well paid and in high demand. The costs are the length, the intensity
of doing coursework, placements and a doctoral thesis simultaneously, and the very low probability of
admission in any given year.

**After M6046.** Practising clinical psychologist — public mental health, hospitals, community
services, private practice. Strong, secure, well-paid demand across Australia. Research becomes
something you do occasionally rather than a career, and if you later want to lead research you would
need a PhD on top, which is a further 3–4 years. This is the right choice if practice is the goal and
research is an interest rather than a vocation.

## Two options you did not list, both worth knowing about

**4586 — PhD (Clinical Neuropsychology).** Structurally identical to 4585, same February-only intake,
same RTP Fees Offset, same H1/H2A entry, same 2,250-hour registrar route, but the endorsement is in
clinical *neuropsychology*. Given that you already enquired about it, and given that your stated
research interest is app-based cognitive and neuropsychological assessment for addiction and OCD,
this is arguably the closest fit of any program here between what you want to research and what you
would be licensed to do. Cognitive assessment is neuropsychology's core business.

**D6008 — Master of Professional Psychology.** One year, February intake, and the entry requirement
is the interesting part: "a fourth year of psychology study from any course accredited by APAC, which
may be a **Graduate Diploma** or Bachelor's honours degree, with at least a **high credit (65%)**
average in fourth year psychology." That explicitly names a graduate diploma and sets the bar at 65%.
You clear it comfortably. It is the fifth year of the 5+1 pathway: one year of coursework, then a
one-year supervised internship you arrange yourself, then the National Psychology Exam, then general
registration. It gives you registration but *not* an area of practice endorsement.

D6008 matters strategically because it is the only professional program here whose academic bar you
clearly meet. If becoming a registered psychologist quickly is important to you, this is the
realistic route — roughly two years to registration, versus a competitive lottery for 4585 or a
threshold you do not meet for M6046.

Also worth noting: entry thresholds for clinical master's programs vary by provider. Some set the bar
at a WAM of 70 rather than 80. If a clinical master's is what you actually want, Monash is not the
only option and its 80% requirement is at the strict end.

## How I would decide

The question to answer first is not "which course" but **"do I want to see clients?"**

**If the answer is no, or research is clearly the priority:** choose **0047**. It is the only one you
can start at any time, the only one where a supervisor can simply decide to take you, tuition is
free, you may be paid a stipend, and it gets you to a research career fastest. This also aligns with
everything you have told me about wanting to work on app-based assessment and treatment
personalisation. Combine it with the funding strategy in `monash-phd-pathway.md`: target
grant-funded, supervisor-selected stipends rather than the central WAM-ranked round.

**If the answer is yes and research matters just as much:** target **4586** first and **4585**
second, for a February 2028 start, applying in the domestic round around mid-September 2027. Tuition
is free, a stipend is possible, and you finish as both a doctor and a licensed specialist. Go in
knowing these are the most competitive programs in the School, so treat them as a high-value
long shot and keep a fallback.

**If the answer is yes and practice is the real goal:** **M6046 is not currently available to you**
at 70–75. The workable route is **D6008 + the 5+1 internship** for general registration in about two
years, and if you later want clinical endorsement you can pursue a bridging program. Alternatively
look at clinical master's programs elsewhere that set entry at a WAM of 70.

**If you want to keep both doors open**, which is where I suspect you actually are: apply for
**0047** with a strong supervisor and grant-funded project as the primary plan, and put in a 4586 or
4585 application in the same cycle if a slot allows. Note the constraint from the apply page — one
application per scholarship round or admission period — so this needs sequencing rather than
scattergun submission. Under no circumstances let the 4585/4586 lottery be your only plan.

One thing to avoid: doing 0047 first and assuming you can add clinical registration cheaply later.
You cannot. A research PhD earns you no credit toward an accredited professional sequence, so
registration afterwards means a further one to two years of coursework and an internship, paid for
out of pocket. If clinical practice is even a moderate possibility for you, the clinical doctorates
are dramatically more efficient than doing the two separately.

## Deadlines in play right now

- **M6046, 2027 intake:** reported closing around **10 September 2026** — about two weeks away.
  Confirm on the course page. Academically out of reach at 70–75, so noted for completeness.
- **4585 / 4586, February 2027 intake:** international closed 31 July 2026; **domestic closed or
  closing 15 September 2026**. You will not hold PR by then, so February 2027 is almost certainly not
  achievable for these. February 2028 is the realistic target, with a domestic deadline likely around
  mid-September 2027.
- **D6008, 2027 intake:** February start, so applications will be closing around the same period.
  Worth checking immediately if the 5+1 route interests you.
- **0047:** no deadline for admission-only. Scholarship rounds as set out in
  `monash-phd-pathway.md` — domestic R2 closes 31 May 2027 for a July–December 2027 start.
