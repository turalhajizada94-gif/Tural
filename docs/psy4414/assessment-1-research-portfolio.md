# Assessment 1 — Research Portfolio

## Nothing to submit

There is no submission required for the research portfolio. The work has already
been done: Assessment 1 is a placeholder that collects the grades earned across
the research sequence, from PSY4412 through to PSY4414.

## Weighting

The portfolio of already-completed activities constitutes **30% of the final
PSY4414 unit mark**.

## Why it exists

The item is administrative. It gives the grades from the earlier research units a
place to roll forward into, so that a single whole grade can be produced covering
all three research units.

## Action required

None. Direct your effort at Assessment 2 (the results section) and the final
manuscript.
