# Results section — submission checklist

A working checklist assembled from the walkthrough. It is a study aid, not the
official checklist: the two short checklists attached to the assessment slide and
the assessment brief itself are authoritative.

## Everyone

- [ ] Word limit respected — quantitative up to 1,200 words *(verify)*, qualitative up to 2,000 words
- [ ] Analysis output submitted alongside the write-up
- [ ] Participant flow reported, with every drop in numbers accounted for
- [ ] Write-up reflects the data analysis plan **and** the feedback received on it
- [ ] Method and supervisor feedback triangulated
- [ ] Both pre-submission supervision sessions used
- [ ] Sample theses consulted for what is required and where it sits
- [ ] Assessment checklist worked through section by section

## Quantitative

- [ ] Data cleaning documented: non-viable responses (passive or very fast responders), reverse scoring, any other transformations
- [ ] All assumption testing for the chosen analysis reported — none omitted
- [ ] Descriptive statistics reported
- [ ] Means reported where mediation or moderation is involved
- [ ] Intercorrelations between study variables reported
- [ ] Inferential statistics written up following PSY4401 conventions
- [ ] Tables, models, and diagrams included as needed (these do not count towards the word limit)

## Qualitative

- [ ] Themes introduced at the start of the results
- [ ] Theme map included
- [ ] Relationships between themes explained
- [ ] Extracts integrated per the guidance attached to the assessment
- [ ] Analytic lens applied — interpretation across, between, and within participants
- [ ] Wrap-up paragraph that summarises the findings and leads into the discussion
