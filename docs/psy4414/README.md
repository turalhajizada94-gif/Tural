## Quick orientation

- **Assessment 1 (Research Portfolio)** — nothing to submit. It is a placeholder
  that carries forward grades earned from PSY4412 through to PSY4414, and it is
  worth 30% of the final PSY4414 unit mark.
- **Assessment 2 (Results Section)** — a competency hurdle. Every project must
  submit an analysis output alongside the write-up, and every project needs a
  participant flow that accounts for attrition.

## A note on sourcing

Two kinds of document are mixed here. The **requirements** page is drawn from
official unit materials: the marking rubric, the GDPA submission guidelines (v4),
the Results Section FAQ, and the Results Section Workbook. Everything else derives
from an auto-transcribed walkthrough video, where unit codes and figures were
spoken rather than written; anything ambiguous in the audio is flagged inline with
a "verify" note.

Where the two disagree, the official documents win, and the Moodle assessment page
and your supervisor override both.
